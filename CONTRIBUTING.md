# Contributing

Issues and pull requests are welcome.

1. Fork [the repo](https://github.com/Suraj64x/AppleMusicActivity).
2. Open `iMusicActivity.sln` in Visual Studio (Windows 11, .NET 10 SDK).
3. Keep the change small and say why it exists.

Bug reports: include Apple Music version, iMusicActivity version, region, and logs from `%localappdata%\iMusicActivity`.
